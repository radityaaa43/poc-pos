Bytes are read from the fake WebUSB printer (`support/webusb.js`), so the real send path runs. Its faults (a failing transfer or claim, unplugging), the user agent and the Wake Lock are stubs too: the real driver lock, USB errors and wake-lock behaviour are manual checks below. The browser's timers, the printer hardware and the USB driver do not.

Manual checks (REQ-004, K1):
- A real printer on Android Chrome over USB-OTG: the ticket prints, is legible, and is cut.
- A real printer on Windows Chrome (WinUSB driver): same; the driver-lock message is covered by K2 below.
- A visible and a hidden second tab on the cashier PC for ten minutes with Cetak otomatis on: tickets still arrive within 10 seconds, or the hidden tab's throttling is noted (the docs say to use a separate window).
- Two granted printers on one PC (the receipt printer and the kitchen printer): receipts stay on the receipt printer, tickets go to the kitchen printer.
- A DIBATALKAN ticket on a real printer: the cancelled quantity and menu are legible at 58 mm and 80 mm, and it is cut (K3b; the e2e reads decoded text from the fake printer, not paper).
- A backlog after a long outage (more than 30 orders) prints in order, one whole order at a time (covered by `KitchenTicketTest`; never seen on hardware).

Manual checks (REQ-004, K2):
- A real Windows PC with a printer still on its default driver: Hubungkan printer shows the driver-lock message with the five steps; after the Zadig WinUSB swap and a reload it connects and prints. The e2e fakes the NetworkError, so the real error and the user-agent heuristic are not proved.
- Paper out is not detectable over WebUSB: with the paper cover open or the roll empty the send still succeeds, no warning shows, and Cetak ulang is the staff's remedy (REQ-004 decision 11). Never promised, never tested.
- A cable pulled in the middle of a ticket: the app shows "Printer dapur tidak terhubung", plugging it back prints the whole ticket again, and the torn stub on paper is for staff to discard.
- The Wake Lock on an Android Chrome tablet: with Cetak otomatis on, the screen stays on past its normal timeout; with it off the screen sleeps again. The e2e only counts calls to a stub.
- A tablet that really sleeps with orders waiting: on waking it shows "Printer dapur offline atau tab tidur" and prints them once. The e2e holds the ticket requests instead.
