# 25-cashier-shifts

| Steps | Roles |
|---|---|
| `01-pos-blocked-without-shift`: POS locked without a shift, shift-gated endpoint bounces, open from the POS; `02-open-shift-validation`: second open keeps the first shift; `03-cash-in-out`: cash in/out change expected cash; `04-close-shift`: close with counted cash and difference, POS locked again; `05-force-close-by-manager`: password step-up (wrong, then right) then force close; a cashier gets 404 on another user's shift; `06-close-blocked` (REQ-003 AC-12): food not handed over is listed under Belum selesai (order, label, amount), the cashier has no force close and the close is refused with the shift still open; `07-close-pay-later-credit` (AC-17): a credit customer's pay later closes, a guest's holds; `08-force-close-held-shift` (AC-13): the manager must write a note, items become not collected, the unpaid bill is booked in the cash difference and left out of non-cash sales, audit entries; `09-close-cancels-unpaid-pay-now` (AC-20): the waiting QRIS sale is cancelled, both stock columns restored and audited, the paid one untouched; `10-two-shifts-one-outlet` (AC-27): another cashier's food, bill, waiting sale and partly paid split neither hold the close nor change; `11-close-held-by-partly-paid-split` (#396 BR-1, BR-4, BR-5): a split with cash taken and the transfer waiting is listed with the amount owed in Indonesian and English, and the close is refused; `12-force-close-keeps-split-payable` (#396 BR-2, BR-6): the manager's force close names it in the audit and leaves it payable, non-cash counts none of the waiting part, the later confirmation is marked *Dibayar setelah shift ditutup*; `13-unpaid-pay-later-not-non-cash` (#396 BR-7): a guest's unpaid pay-later sale is not in non-cash sales, and settled after the force close it moves to the paying shift and is marked; `14-close-held-by-pending-discount` (#542 BR-1, BR-2, BR-4): the pending discount is listed with its order, label and discount amount (read-only for the cashier), the manager approves it from the list and the row disappears so the close goes through, and another shift's pending sale is neither listed nor touched; `15-force-close-leaves-discount-pending` (#542 BR-3): a force close with a note leaves the sale and its approval request pending and the cash taken in the recap, the later denial on the approvals page leaves the stored recap unchanged | cashier, manager |

Not covered here (PHPUnit): the cancel of failed and expired sales, returned sales, a split with a failed gateway part or paid by a gateway webhook after the close, payment of a booked bill in a later shift. The disabled close button while a sale waits to sync is proved in `30-offline-sale/06` (the sync is blocked, the page stays online).

Manual (needs a real device): printing the X and Z shift reports on a thermal printer.
