AC-02 and AC-09 are driven through `POST /dine-order/{token}/pay` and the `dine.order-state` query (the refusal texts and the 429 of the pay panel are Vitest cases in `GuestPayPanel.test.jsx`). The cashier steps (AC-06, AC-08, AC-10) sign in as the cashier of their own `dine.bill` (`staff=1`: POS, her shift, History and the sales report of that outlet only, so counts and totals there are exact) and send the gateway's signed notification (`midtransNotification` in `support/dine-bill.js`); the shift is force closed through the real shift page (by a `shiftclose.manager` of that outlet) so the snapshot is real. Not covered here: true concurrency (a settlement and a cashier payment in the same millisecond, a close and a settlement together); PHPUnit proves the order of the locks and the MySQL CI job runs the same tests.

Why folder 80: the `dine.bill` scenario creates an outlet and a warehouse per call, and the super admin's lists show every warehouse as a column (`50-raw-materials/03` reads the last column of its row). The folder runs after every feature that reads such lists, like `75`-`79`, whose `public.sale` outlets are the precedent. Keep this folder after them.

Manual checks (no sandbox in CI): a real Midtrans and a real Xendit sandbox charge (does Midtrans Snap return a `qr_string` for the restricted-methods charge, does the finish redirect land on the status page, is the 15 minute expiry applied), and a real e-wallet scan. For the cashier steps: a real sandbox run of a takeover (the cashier takes cash, then the guest pays the old QR) and of a late guest payment, on Midtrans and on Xendit, and a cashier's own QRIS after a guest attempt (it must settle as booked). For the expiry of a superseded charge (#359): a real sandbox run on a pending Midtrans QRIS charge and on a pending Xendit invoice, and on a Midtrans page with no method picked (Core API 404, then the Snap session cancel, whose sandbox URL the docs do not show).
