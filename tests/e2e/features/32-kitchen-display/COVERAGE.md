# 32-kitchen-display

| Steps | Roles |
|---|---|
| `00-kitchen-intake`: a paid mixed cart sends only the flagged line to the kitchen as accepted; a pending transfer stays out until confirmed paid; a failed and expired payments send nothing, and paying the open bill later (even twice) sends it once (REQ-003 AC-02, AC-05, AC-19 server half; read back through the `kitchen.items` arrange scenario, since there is no kitchen screen yet) | cashier, manager |
| `01-one-queue`: a paid dine-in QR order and a paid POS sale appear in one queue with table name or invoice, order type and item, oldest first (@REQ-003-AC-06); `02-paid-only`: a bank-transfer sale waiting for confirmation is absent, and appears on the open screen once a manager confirms it (@REQ-003-AC-02); `03-mixed-cart`: a cart with food and a packaged product shows only the lines flagged "needs preparation" (@REQ-003-AC-05); `04-live-update`: a sale paid in another browser context appears on the open screen without a reload (@REQ-003-AC-07); `05-ten-seconds-and-language`: a sale made after the screen opened shows within 10 seconds, and the screen switches to English (@REQ-003-AC-24); `06-access`: a role without prepare or handover gets 403 and no sidebar entry; `07-start-and-ready`: Mulai buat then Siap on one item, and Semua siap on a second order (@REQ-003-AC-08); `08-prepare-permission`: a handover-only user sees the queue without step buttons and a direct POST gets 403 (@REQ-003-AC-10); `09-concurrent-tap`: two people tap Mulai buat on the same item, the second sees "Sudah diproses oleh" with the first one's name (@REQ-003-AC-23); `07-intake-recovery`: a paid sale whose intake never ran (arranged by `kitchen.paid-without-intake`) is absent from the screen, and appears once after `kitchen:recover-intake` runs, even if it runs twice (@REQ-003-AC-02) | cashier, manager, stock-opname, own users (05, 08) |

Manual checks: the offline banner ("Koneksi terputus") and a session that ends while the screen is open (covered by Vitest and `KitchenDisplayTest`); the offline tap refusal ("Gagal menyimpan", covered by Vitest); touch use on a kitchen tablet; a screen left open all day.

Printing the tickets of this screen (print device, paper, Cetak otomatis) is covered in `33-kitchen-printer`.
