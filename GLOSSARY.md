# Glossary

The domain vocabulary of poc-pos. Code, docs, issues and UI use these terms exactly; when
two words compete for one concept, pick one here and use it everywhere. `/grill-with-docs` and
`/domain-modeling` add terms as they get resolved.

| Term | Meaning | Not to be confused with | In code |
|---|---|---|---|
| Outlet | A branch of the business (e.g. one coffee shop of a chain) that can sell when sales are enabled | Warehouse: an outlet owns one or more warehouses | `Outlet` / `outlets` (`code`, `is_sales_enabled`) |
| Warehouse | A stock location. `PUSAT` is the central, non-sales warehouse (`type = main`) | Outlet: stock is held per warehouse, not per outlet | `Warehouse` / `warehouses` |
| Warehouse stock | Operational stock of one product in one warehouse; the source of truth | Global stock | `ProductWarehouse` / `product_warehouse.stock` |
| Global stock | Sum of a product's warehouse stock, kept in sync in the same transaction | Warehouse stock | `products.stock` |
| Product | Anything sold or stocked, with buy and sell price in whole rupiah | Unit: a product can be sold in several units | `Product` / `products` |
| Composite product (recipe) | A product made of component products; selling it deducts its components' stock. A recipe short of one portion stays listed as sold out (Habis) on the POS and the QR menu, not hidden | Bundle pricing rule: a price, not a stock recipe | `products.is_composite`, `composite_product_items`, `Product::stockIn` / `stockAtOutlet` |
| Unit / conversion factor | A unit a product is stocked or sold in, and how many base units it equals | Unit of a raw material in a recipe | `Unit`, `ProductUnit` / `product_units.conversion_factor` |
| Raw material | A product flagged `is_raw_material`: stocked and bought, never sold on the POS. Managed on Master Data → Bahan Baku, where "purchased by" says whether the central warehouse (`central`) or each outlet (`outlet`) buys it | Composite product: made of components and sold | `products.is_raw_material`, `products.purchased_by` |
| Needs preparation | A product flagged `needs_preparation`: the kitchen makes it to order, with an optional prep target in minutes (`prep_target_minutes`). A composite product starts flagged on release, but the two are independent and either can be changed per product. Each sale line freezes the flag when it is sold (`transaction_details.needs_preparation`; null on lines from before, which read the product) | Composite product: that is a stock recipe, this is a kitchen flag | `products.needs_preparation`, `transaction_details.needs_preparation`, `TransactionDetail::goesToKitchen()`, `products.prep_target_minutes` |
| Kitchen item | A sale line whose product needs preparation, with its status trail (accepted, preparing, ready, served, cancelled by a completed sales return, or not collected after a forced shift close) and who moved it and when (`preparing_at`/`preparing_by`, `ready_at`/`ready_by`, `served_at`/`served_by`, set by the kitchen display steps: Mulai buat, Siap, Serahkan). Created once the sale is paid | Dine order: that is the QR order, this is one line of a paid sale | `KitchenItem` / `kitchen_items` |
| Kitchen station | A named place in the kitchen (Bar, Panggang) that a product needing preparation is assigned to; managed on Settings → Stasiun dapur with `products-edit`, shop-wide, names unique ignoring case. A kitchen item copies its product's station when it is created; deleting a station is a soft delete that moves its products to Dapur and leaves existing items alone | Outlet: a branch; stations are inside the kitchen of every outlet | `KitchenStation` / `kitchen_stations`, `products.kitchen_station_id`, `kitchen_items.kitchen_station_id` |
| Semua stasiun | The kitchen display setting that shows every station's items: each order whole, the station name on each item and "x dari y siap" per order. It is the default of every device; a device set to one or more stations shows only their items. The choice is kept in that browser (`localStorage` key `kitchenStations`), never on the server | Kitchen station: that is one place, this is the unfiltered view | `Pages/Dashboard/Kitchen/Index.jsx` |
| Dapur (default station) | The one station that always exists (`is_default`), created by the migration. It can be renamed, never deleted. A product with no station, or whose station was deleted, goes to Dapur; the product form stores Dapur as no station (`null`) | Kitchen display ("Layar Dapur"): that is the screen, this is a station | `KitchenStation::DEFAULT_NAME`, `KitchenStation::defaultId()` |
| Kitchen display | The read-only screen (`/dashboard/kitchen`) listing the open kitchen items of the current outlet, grouped per order, oldest first, refreshed every 5 seconds. Needs `dine-orders-prepare` or `dine-orders-handover` | Dine orders page: that is where staff accept and settle QR orders | `Kitchen/Index.jsx`, `KitchenController`, route `kitchen.index` |
| Print device | A device (tablet or PC) with the kitchen display open that prints kitchen tickets. It has a name unique in the outlet, saved on the server, and its printer, paper size and auto-print saved in that browser. Each print device prints an item once and prints only items that reached the kitchen after it was named. In the UI it is "Nama perangkat cetak" / "Print device name" | Kitchen display: that is the screen, the print device is one device's printing settings; Kitchen station: a place in the kitchen where a product is made, not a device | `KitchenPrintDevice` / `kitchen_print_devices`, `PrintDevicePanel.jsx`, `useKitchenPrintDevice.js` |
| Kitchen ticket | The slip a print device prints for one order: table name, order name or invoice number, order type, time, and each line with quantity and note, no prices; BELUM LUNAS for a pay-later order. An add-on order has its own ticket: the original's number, "TAMBAHAN" / "ADD-ON" under it, only the added lines. One row per print device and item records that it was printed | Receipt: the customer's slip with prices | `KitchenTicketService`, `buildKitchenTicketBytes`, `kitchen_ticket_prints` |
| Order name | The optional name or buzzer number (max 20 characters) a cashier types at POS checkout; shown on the receipt and used as the kitchen number of that sale. "Nama pesanan" in the UI | Table name: a table order has no order name; Invoice: the sale's number, shown when there is no name | `transactions.order_name`, `OrderDetails.jsx`, `KitchenOrders` |
| Cancellation ticket (DIBATALKAN) | The slip a print device prints when a completed sales return cancels a kitchen item whose ticket it printed: the order number, the returned quantity and the menu, no prices. A draft return prints none. Printed once per print device, within 12 hours of the return. In the UI it is "DIBATALKAN" / "CANCELLED" | Kitchen ticket: the original slip for a new order; the Dibatalkan badge: what the display shows for 2 minutes | `KitchenTicketService::pendingCancellations`, `buildKitchenTicketBytes` (`kind: cancel`), `kitchen_cancellations`, `kitchen_cancellation_prints` |
| Belum tercetak | The chip on a kitchen display card whose ticket has not printed: it failed, or has waited more than 30 seconds. Only the browser of a print device with Cetak otomatis on knows it; nothing is stored. It clears when the original ticket prints. In the UI "Belum tercetak" / "Not printed" | Belum lunas: the order is not paid | `isNotPrinted` in `printerStatus.js`, `Kitchen/Index.jsx` |
| Cetak ulang | A reprint of a kitchen ticket from the order's card, marked CETAK ULANG / "REPRINT". Offered only for an order this print device has printed, any number of times, and it records nothing. A card that has not printed offers "Coba lagi" instead, which prints the normal ticket and records it. In the UI "Cetak ulang" / "Reprint" | Cetak percobaan: the test print in the printer settings; Coba lagi: the retry of a ticket that never printed | `reprint` in `useKitchenPrintDevice.js`, `buildKitchenTicketBytes` (`reprint`) |
| Not collected (Tidak diambil) | A kitchen item still not handed over when its shift was force-closed. Final, and left out of the speed report. In the UI it is "Tidak diambil" / "Not collected" | Cancelled: removed by a sales return, with its stock handled there | `KitchenItem::STATUS_NOT_COLLECTED` |
| Purchase unit | The unit a product is bought in. The default is the `product_units` row with `is_purchase_default`; its `buy_price` is the price per that unit | Base unit: the unit stock is kept in | `product_units.is_purchase_default`, `product_units.buy_price` |
| Batch | A received lot of a product in a warehouse, with optional expiry; consumed first-expiry-first | Stock mutation | `ProductBatch` / `product_batches` |
| Stock mutation | One logged stock change (sale, receiving, transfer, opname, return) with before/after quantities | Batch | `StockMutation` / `stock_mutations` |
| Stock opname | A physical stock count that sets warehouse stock to the counted quantity | Stock mutation: opname creates adjustment mutations | `StockOpname` / `stock_opnames` |
| Stock transfer | Moving stock between warehouses: draft → in transit → completed or cancelled | Goods receiving | `StockTransfer` / `stock_transfers` |
| Purchase order (PO) | An order to a supplier | Goods receiving: what actually arrived | `PurchaseOrder` / `purchase_orders` |
| Goods receiving | Recording what arrived from a supplier; adds stock and creates a payable | Purchase order | `GoodsReceiving` / `goods_receivings` |
| Payable / receivable | Money the business owes a supplier / is owed by a customer | Transaction | `Payable`, `Receivable` |
| May buy on credit ("Boleh piutang") | A customer flag: pay later may become a receivable with a due date. Only users with `customers-credit-approve` change it; each change is audit-logged. Only such a customer can be given a credit limit; switching the flag off keeps a stored limit, unused | Receivable: the debt itself; credit limit: how much of it is allowed | `customers.may_buy_on_credit` |
| Credit limit ("Batas piutang") | The most a customer who may buy on credit should owe, in whole rupiah (0 or more; 0 = no credit). Set on the customer page with `customers-credit-approve`; each change is audit-logged. Checkout (web and API) refuses a credit sale when Used plus its total would pass it ("Melebihi batas piutang — sisa Rp X", `credit_limit_exceeded`), so a limit set below Used blocks new credit at once; otherwise it refuses while a receivable is overdue ("Lunasi piutang yang lewat jatuh tempo dulu", `credit_overdue`) | May buy on credit: whether they may owe at all | `customers.credit_limit`, `CustomerCreditService` |
| No limit ("Tanpa batas") | A customer who may buy on credit and has an empty credit limit: every such customer when the limit was released. Checkout never refuses them, overdue or not | A limit of Rp 0, which allows no credit | `customers.credit_limit` is null |
| Used ("Terpakai") | The balance left on all of a customer's unpaid receivables across all outlets, computed on read, never stored. "Sisa" is the limit minus it, never below 0 | The receivables list's rows, which are limited to the viewer's outlets | `Receivable::scopeOutstanding` |
| Near the limit ("Mendekati batas") | Used is at least 80% of the credit limit (and above 0); includes customers over the limit in the filter | Over the limit | `ReceivableService::creditView` |
| Over the limit ("Melewati batas") | Used is more than the credit limit; with a limit of Rp 0, any amount owed | Overdue receivable: past its due date with a balance, whatever the limit | `ReceivableService::creditView` |
| Pay later ("Bayar Belakangan") | An outlet setting, off until the outlet turns it on: a sale is made now and paid later, marked "Belum lunas", and goes to the kitchen at once. A receivable with a due date is created only for a customer who may buy on credit; a guest bill needs a note of at least 2 characters. Also offered on the table-QR menu (`dine_orders.payment_option = pay_later`) when the setting is on: staff accept it, its kitchen items are recorded then, unpaid. Its status page shows "Belum lunas" and, at an outlet with a ready gateway, the guest can pay it online (a guest payment attempt); paying records no kitchen item again. Not available offline. Changed on Settings → Outlets with `outlets-update`, audit-logged | Unpaid bill: a sale waiting to be settled | `settings.pay_later_enabled` (per `outlet_id`), `transactions.is_pay_later` |
| Transaction | A completed sale (invoice) with its lines and tenders | Dine order: its transaction is created at accept and paid later (Belum Lunas) | `Transaction`, `TransactionDetail` |
| Dine order | A dine-in table order, paid at the counter | Transaction | `DineOrder` / `dine_orders` |
| Offline sale ("Transaksi offline") | A POS sale made without a connection, held in the device's queue until the server takes it. Until then it is pending ("Transaksi offline tertunda"); removing one from the device is audit-logged (`offline_sale.discarded`) and changes nothing on the server | Unpaid bill: a sale the server already has, still waiting for payment | IndexedDB `pending_transactions`, `offlineQueue.js` |
| Add-on order | Items added to an order that is not finished (finished: the original and every non-cancelled add-on paid, nothing left in the kitchen): a sale of its own (payment, stock, shift, promotions), linked to the root order. Made with "Tambah pesanan" on a POS receipt or a table-QR status page; its receipt names the original ("Tambahan untuk …" / "Add-on to …"). Its order number is the original's | Cart edit: the original sale is never rewritten | `AddOnOrderService`, `transactions.parent_transaction_id`, `dine_orders.add_on_to_dine_order_id` |
| Unpaid bill ("Belum Lunas") | A sale still waiting for payment: an accepted dine order whose transaction is `unpaid`, or a guest / non-credit pay-later POS sale (`transactions.is_pay_later`, no receivable). Settled in full, with any method, from the POS "Belum Lunas" section or Transactions History (both offer **Bayar** for a bill whose gateway claim was abandoned, while a live claim keeps **Lihat QR**); after a partial return the amount is the remaining total, a fully returned bill is not listed, and when two cashiers settle at once the first wins. Not a credit sale: no receivable. A dine order has its own fulfillment status (`accepted`, `preparing`, `ready`, `served`) apart from the payment status of its transaction; payment comes first (the kitchen starts only on a paid bill, "Menunggu Pembayaran" until then; a table-QR pay-later bill is made while unpaid, and its guest page shows "Belum lunas" and can pay online) and the order is `completed` when it is `served` | Receivable (pay later) | `Transaction::scopeOpenBills`, `payment_method = open_bill` |
| Cashier shift | A cashier's open-to-close session with opening and closing cash | | `CashierShift` |
| HPP (cost of goods sold; "harga pokok penjualan") | Cost of what was sold, frozen on each sale line from the selling warehouse's average cost; sale minus HPP is profit | Buy price: the current price, not the cost at sale time | `transaction_details.cost_total` |
| Average cost ("harga pokok persediaan" / "biaya rata-rata") | Weighted-average cost per stock unit of one product in one warehouse; moves on goods receiving (and starts at the reference price on opening stock), other warehouses do not change | Buy price: the reference price, the latest price paid, also the fallback until a warehouse has an average | `product_warehouse.avg_cost`, `InventoryCostService` |
| Backlog item | A requirement file `backlog/REQ-<n>-<slug>.md` with `AC-<n>` acceptance criteria | Issue: the unit of work built from a backlog item | `backlog/` |
| Payment attempt | One try to collect the online payment of a sale; a retry after a failed or expired payment starts the next attempt, with gateway order id `{invoice}-r{n}` (a retried sale's charge carries it too, and every gateway charge a cashier makes on a dine-in or pay-later bill is a new attempt). The counter only grows: a cashier's payment on a sale with attempts also moves it. A paid callback of any other attempt is a late payment: booked as the payment only when the bill is not paid, no pending or paid tender holds it (a failed or expired one does not) and the amount is what is still owed, otherwise flagged "Bayar ganda — refund manual" and never booked | Payment status: the sale's state (`pending`, `paid`, `failed`, ...), not one try | `transactions.payment_attempt` |
| Guest payment attempt | A payment attempt that the guest starts from the order status page (Bayar sekarang) for the bill's remaining total, computed by the server. It never changes the bill's cashier or shift, creates no tender row, and a later press or a retry replaces it. A pending bill that has tender rows is the cashier's own charge, not a guest payment attempt | Cashier's payment: taken at the counter with `dine-orders.pay`, books the bill to the cashier's shift | `DineOrderOnlinePaymentService`, `transactions.payment_failure_reason` |
| Takeover | A cashier's payment on a bill whose guest online charge is pending (no tender rows). It supersedes the guest's charge by moving the payment attempt, in the same transaction as the payment; a queued job then asks the gateway to expire that charge, and a guest payment that still arrives is flagged for a manual refund (booked if the cashier's own gateway charge failed and nothing else holds the bill). Audit `dine_order.online_payment_taken_over` | The cashier's own pending gateway charge or transfer: that one still holds the bill | `DineOrderService::pay`, `Transaction::isAwaitingGuestPayment` |
| Paid after shift closed ("Dibayar setelah shift ditutup") | A payment that settles a sale after its owning shift closed, when the closed recap did not hold that money: a gateway payment of an unpaid bill (Belum Lunas) or gateway sale, the unreceived part of a split sale (webhook or confirmed transfer), a pay-later sale without a receivable (webhook or settlement at the till, which moves the sale to the paying shift), or a confirmed transfer of a bill a forced close booked. A dine-in bill paid at the till after its shift closed (cash, takeover, transfer or gateway) is marked too, and so is a pay-later sale retried in cash from the print page after its shift closed; the audit's `source` says which path. The closed recap is never changed; the sale gets `paid_after_close_at` and the audit entry `payment.paid_after_close` | Late payment: money that is not booked | `transactions.paid_after_close_at`, `CashierShiftService::countedInClosedRecap` |
| Partly paid split sale | A Bayar sekarang split sale with at least one tender paid that is not paid as a whole. It holds its shift's close with the amount still owed, a forced close leaves it payable, and the recap counts only the money received | Unpaid Pay now sale: no money received, cancelled when the shift closes. A partly paid bill (Bayar Belakangan or dine-in) holds as a bill | `Transaction::scopePartlyPaidSplit`, `ShiftCloseBlockerService` |
| Overpayment after return | The part of a payment beyond what a sale owes after the returns completed before it was paid (a return made while a gateway charge or transfer waited). The sale is paid, the excess is flagged "Bayar ganda — refund manual" and refunded by hand, audit `payment.overpaid_after_return`; the shift recap leaves it out of the sales | Late payment: money that is not booked at all | `transactions.overpaid_amount`, `Transaction::owedAfterReturns`, `TransactionTenderService::flagOverpayment` |
| Awaiting online payment ("Menunggu pembayaran online") | The label the POS unpaid bill ("Belum Lunas") section puts on a dine-in bill while the guest's online charge is pending (no tender rows). The cashier can still take payment (takeover) and is told first to ask the guest whether they already paid | The cashier's own pending gateway charge or transfer: shown as "Lihat QR", no label | `transactions.open-bills`, `Transaction::isAwaitingGuestPayment` |
| Kitchen speed report | Read-only report "Laporan Kecepatan Dapur": per menu item and kitchen station (filterable by station) the orders, portions, medians and averages of the make time and the wait + make time, and the share within the target; plus the average time from payment to the last item served, which stays whole-order under a station filter | Kitchen display: the live queue of items to make | `KitchenSpeedReportService` |
| Waktu buat | Make time: from *preparing* to *ready* of one kitchen item; the prep target is compared with it | Waktu tunggu + buat | `kitchen_items.preparing_at` to `ready_at` |
| Waktu tunggu + buat | Wait plus make time: from *accepted* (paid) to *ready* of one kitchen item | Waktu buat | `kitchen_items.accepted_at` to `ready_at` |
| Modifier group ("Grup modifier") | A named set of options (a modifier: size, sugar level, topping), required ("Wajib") or optional ("Opsional"), with a minimum and a maximum number of choices; attached to sellable menus in a display order, never to a raw material; soft deleted. A menu without groups sells as before | Recipe: what the menu itself is made of | `ModifierGroup`, `modifier_groups`, `product_modifier_group`, `docs/features/modifiers.md` |
| Option ("Opsi") | One choice in a modifier group: name, extra price (0 or more, the same at every outlet), optional recipe, default flag (at most one per group, exactly one in a required group); switched off per outlet; soft deleted | A variation sold as its own product | `ModifierOption`, `modifier_options`, `modifier_option_components`, `modifier_option_outlet` |
| Waste | Raw material or recipe menu thrown away, recorded with a reason; a menu takes its recipe ingredients. Statuses: Tercatat (stock taken), Menunggu persetujuan (stock untouched) | Stock opname: a counted variance without a reason | `StockWaste` / `stock_wastes`, `StockWasteService` |
| Waste value | Average cost at the moment the stock is taken x quantity, whole rupiah per stock product taken; an estimate while pending | HPP: the cost of what was sold | `stock_wastes.total_value`, `stock_waste_moves.value` |
| Waste approval limit | Rupiah per waste record, per outlet (default Rp 100,000): at or under it the stock is taken at once, above it the record waits for approval | Credit limit | `settings.waste_approval_limit` (per `outlet_id`) |
| Biaya waste | The waste cost of a period: the stored `total_value` of records recorded in it (Tercatat or Dibatalkan) minus the value of records cancelled in it, so a cancellation reduces its own period; Menunggu persetujuan and Ditolak never count | HPP penjualan: the cost of what was sold | `StockWasteReportService::costTotal` |
| Profit setelah waste | Sales profit (`profit_total`, from the locked HPP) minus Biaya waste; may be negative. Both lines are hidden while an invoice, cashier or customer filter is set | Margin: a percentage on sales only | `ProfitReportController::reportData` |
| Waste report ("Laporan Waste") | Read-only report for one outlet and period: the total Biaya waste, quantity and value per item (a menu as the menu, valued at its recipe ingredients) and record count and value per reason, cancellations subtracted | Stock waste list: what was recorded, one record at a time | `WasteReportController`, `StockWasteReportService` |
| Waste near the limit ("Mendekati batas") | A waste record whose value is at the outlet's approval limit or up to 10% below it; counted per record on its recorded day, using the outlet's current limit (shown on the report) | Credit "near the limit": a customer near the credit limit | `StockWasteReportService::byRecorderAndDay` |
| Waste percent ("Persentase waste") | Net Biaya waste of a period divided by HPP penjualan of the same outlet and period, one decimal; empty without sales. A return lowers HPP only when its goods were restocked | Margin: profit as a percent of revenue | `StockWasteReportService::salesCost` |
| Bill settings ("Tagihan") | The per-outlet rules a bill is computed with: service charge rate, tax type (PPN per product, or PB1), PB1 rate and cash rounding (Tanpa, 100, 500). Changed in the outlet form with `outlets-update`, audit-logged; they apply to new bills only | Tagihan as a bill or an unpaid sale ("Belum lunas") | `BillSettingsService`; `settings` keys `service_charge_rate`, `tax_kind`, `pb1_rate`, `cash_rounding_unit` (per `outlet_id`) |
| Service charge | A percentage an outlet adds to dine-in bills (a POS sale with order type Di Tempat, `in_store`), on the subtotal after discounts; 0 = off | Tax; `shipping_cost` | `settings.service_charge_rate`, `transactions.service_charge`, `transactions.service_charge_rate` |
| PB1 | The regional restaurant tax: one outlet rate on the whole bill, used instead of per-product PPN at an outlet whose tax type is PB1 | PPN: per product | `settings.tax_kind = pb1`, `settings.pb1_rate`, `transactions.tax_kind` |
| PPN (VAT) | The national value-added tax, per product: charged on top of an `exclusive` price, or already inside an `inclusive` one. An inclusive price's PPN is stored in `transactions.tax_included` and reported in `tax_total`, never added to `grand_total` again; sales from before #576 keep the figures they were charged | PB1: the outlet's one restaurant tax instead of per-product PPN | `transactions.tax_total`, `transactions.tax_included`, `BillTotalService::compute` |
| Reservasi (reservation) | One booking of one dine-in table for a guest at a date and time, recorded by staff. Starts as Dicatat; two on one table must be at least 2 hours apart | Dine order: what the guest orders once there | `DineReservation` / `dine_reservations` |
| Dipesan | A table with a Dicatat reservation, from 30 minutes before its time until staff mark it. Derived, never stored; showing it on the table list is not built yet (#426) | Dicatat: the reservation's own status | `DineReservation::bookedSoon` |
| Datang | The mark for a reservation whose guest arrived. It still holds its time slot | Tidak datang: the guest did not come, which frees the slot (like Batal) | `status = arrived` |
| Tidak datang | The mark for a reservation whose guest did not come | Batal: the booking was called off | `status = no_show` |

## Retired terms

Words a decision replaced. UI text, docs, issues and plans use the replacement. Code identifiers
that predate the change (`METHOD_OPEN_BILL`, `payment_method = open_bill`) keep their names.

| Retired | Use instead | Since |
|---|---|---|
| "Tagihan Terbuka", "open bill" | "Belum Lunas", "unpaid bill" | 2026-11-25, REQ-003 decision 4 |
| "diracik", "racik" | "Perlu dibuat di dapur" | 2026-11-25, REQ-003 |
| "stasiun cetak", "print station" | "perangkat cetak", "print device" | 2026-11-26, REQ-004 decision 10 |
